Welcome to ICAPST 2026

The Department of Physics, University of Rajasthan, is proud to host and welcome you at ICAPST, a distinguished platform for all scientists, academicians, scholars, and industrialists to share recent fundamental and technological developments in the diverse field of physical sciences. The conference will provide an exciting opportunity to present ideas, the latest research, and discuss science, technology, policy, and associated critical challenges. The conference encourages young scholars/students to present their research findings, offering a rich opportunity to interact with eminent speakers/experts, project developers, researchers, and scientists. With an emphasis on both fundamental and applied research, the conference's vision is to connect and explore scientific advances and technologies in support of a global mission for sustainable development.

Be a Part of ICAPST 2026 – Where Knowledge Advances & Research Inspires

Conference Attractions

  • Interdisciplinary Scientific Sessions covering Physics, Chemistry, Materials Science, Nanotechnology, Energy, Environment, and Emerging Technologies. Keynote and Invited Lectures on cutting-edge research and future technological trends.
  • Abstract Publication in Conference Proceedings.
  • Selected papers may be recommended for publication in Scopus/SCI-indexed journals/proceedings: Solar Compass (Elsevier), Physica Status Solidi B (PSS B), Annalen der Physik (ANDP) and Energy Technology (ENTE)
  • ICAPST Awards
  • Fee concessions are available for foreign students/scholars, and special discounted Conference Day Tickets are offered for Indian UG/PG students.
  • Experience the rich heritage of Jaipur, known as the Pink City.

Journal Publication

The selected papers from ICAPST 2026 will be recommended for publication in the following journals, as per the conference tracks:

Solar Compass — Elsevier

Publishes cutting-edge research in solar energy science and technology.

Physica Status Solidi B (PSS B)

An international journal devoted to solid state physics.

Annalen der Physik (ANDP)

One of the oldest and most renowned physics journals globally.

Energy Technology (ENTE)

Focuses on applied energy research and engineering science.
